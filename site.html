I'm a first-year mechatronics engineering student at GCET who learns by building. I've made a 17-degree-of-freedom humanoid robot and a custom digital camera, designed a 3D-printed FPV drone, led student communities in robotics and rocketry, and I'm working through the e-Yantra Robotics Competition. In parallel I'm building QuickFIX, a platform that makes hiring a verified home-repair professional safe, fast, and fair on price.

I care about the small details that make technology feel trustworthy: knowing who is at your door, when they'll arrive, and what things really cost. Outside engineering, I shoot night and long-exposure moon photos.
